128 assignments to new and usual for each of Bergen, Oslo and Trondheim, in random blocks of 4 and 6 (R blockrand::blockrand()); each block holds every group equally often.
Each list was 40 places long at least: the last block is completed, so a list can be a little longer (here 42, 44). Use the places in order, from number 1.
Keep the list away from the people who recruit, so nobody knows the next group in advance (allocation concealment).
with_seed=1 makes the same list again with R 4.6.2 or newer (R's sample() changed in R 3.6.0).
