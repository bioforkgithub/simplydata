Example tables you can use (made up for practice, except bcg and passive_smoking, real published studies, ability_tests, real published test scores, sea_levels, real published sea levels, and sampled_schools, a real survey sample):

  sdata.get_example("marks")
      Test marks of 18 made-up children in three classes.
      Columns: name, class, subject, hours, marks, height

  sdata.get_example("weather")
      Weather in three made-up cities, month by month.
      Columns: month, month_number, city, temperature, rain, wind_direction, wind_speed

  sdata.get_example("fruit")
      Fruit sold by two made-up shops in one week. One row is one sale.
      Columns: day, shop, fruit, count, price

  sdata.get_example("growth")
      Population of four made-up countries, every two years from 2000 to 2024.
      Columns: year, country, population, forest

  sdata.get_example("sensor")
      Five seconds of a made-up sensor, 200 readings per second: cycles, a hum, a drift, noise and spikes.
      Columns: time, signal, vibration

  sdata.get_example("visitors")
      Monthly visitors to two made-up museums, 2014 to 2025: a rising trend, a yearly season and noise.
      Columns: month, museum, visitors

  sdata.get_example("river")
      Daily water level and nitrate at two made-up river stations, 2024 to 2025. Some days and cells are missing.
      Columns: date, station, level, nitrate

  sdata.get_example("shop")
      Daily sales of two made-up shops from January to March 2026. Some cells are empty.
      Columns: date, shop, sales, customers

  sdata.get_example("school")
      Made-up pupils in 12 schools, three classes in each: study, sleep and test marks. Some sleep cells are empty.
      Columns: school, pupil, class, hours, sleep, breakfast, marks, passed, days_absent
