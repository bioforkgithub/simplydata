The Mann-Kendall test and the interval of the slope assume independent readings; with positive autocorrelation the p-value is too small and the interval too narrow. simplydata has no correction for this (the modified Mann-Kendall test of Hamed & Rao 1998 is in the R package modifiedmk); read them with care.
Method: Sen's slope with its 95 % interval, as R trend::sens.slope() (SciPy theilslopes); Mann-Kendall test with continuity correction, as R trend::mk.test() (run in R). Both assume independent readings; checked with the lag-1 autocorrelation of the readings left after Sen's line (statsmodels acf, acorr_ljungbox(lags=[1])).
