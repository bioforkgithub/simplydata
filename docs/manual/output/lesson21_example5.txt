Model averages of "marks" for each "breakfast" (180 rows), with "hours" held at its average (5.00) and the groups of "school" counted equally:
  no: average 46.6 (95 % CI 44.9 to 48.3)
  yes: average 49.5 (95 % CI 48.3 to 50.8)
Each pair of groups (difference, one pair, so nothing to adjust for):
  no minus yes: difference -2.95 (95 % CI -5.13 to -0.768); about 1 of 100 studies would show this much if the two groups truly did not differ (p = 0.008).
Method: the model lm(marks ~ hours + school + breakfast), then R emmeans::emmeans(m, ~ breakfast) and pairs().
Note: "breakfast" was not in using=, so it was added to the model.
