The formula (x + 2)**2 - 4, with the letter x.
(x + 2)**2 - 4 multiplied out: x**2 + 4*x   (SymPy expand)
x**2 + 4*x as a product: x*(x + 4)   (SymPy factor)
The slope of x*(x + 4) as x changes: 2*x + 4   (the derivative; SymPy diff)
x*(x + 4) at x = 3: 21   (SymPy subs and evalf)
x**2 = 2 when x = -sqrt(2) or x = sqrt(2)   (SymPy solve, exact)
These are all the solutions (SymPy solveset gives the same set).
