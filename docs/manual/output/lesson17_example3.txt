Made 2 clusters of 30 rows from 10 columns (clover, daisy, buttercup, dandelion, nettle, thistle and 4 more) (each put on the same scale first): sizes 14 and 16 (clusters 1 and 2, numbered in the order they first appear). 24.4 % of the spread lies between the clusters (between-cluster / total sum of squares); the rest is within them.
Average silhouette width 0.20: no real structure (Kaufman & Rousseeuw's guide: above 0.70 strong, 0.51 to 0.70 reasonable, 0.26 to 0.50 weak and could be made by the method, 0.25 or less no real structure; R cluster::silhouette). Clusters always appear, even in random data.
Method: k-means (Hartigan-Wong) with 25 random starts, run in R: set.seed(1); kmeans(x, 2, nstart = 25). Added the column "cluster".
