The average "sleep" is 7.72 in no and 8.20 in yes: no is 0.485 lower (95 % CI of the difference -0.774 to -0.196).
If there were truly no difference, a difference this big or bigger would turn up in about 1 of 1000 studies like this one (p = 0.001).
Test: Welch's t-test (t = -3.32, df = 145.821), the usual test for two averages; it does not assume both groups have the same spread. The p-value comes from shuffling, not from the t distribution: the group labels were shuffled 9999 times (seed 1); the same seed gives the same p-value (SciPy permutation_test, as R perm::permTS()).
