A design table of 12 plots: the 2 x 2 = 4 combinations of temperature and salinity; 3 randomised blocks: every combination once in each block, in its own random order (R agricolae::design.ab(design = "rcbd")).
Work through the plots in the order of "plot"; the randomisation spreads unknown differences (soil, position, time of day) evenly over the treatments.
"standard order" numbers the combinations as R expand.grid() lists them (the first factor changes fastest), the order of design textbooks.
with_seed=2 makes the same table again (R's Mersenne-Twister generator, as after set.seed() in R 4.6.2; agricolae's own default generator, Super-Duper, gives another layout).
